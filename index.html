Make & pop. Tap a bell or empty water for pearly bubbles. Touch a bubble again to pop it and nourish the reef. Each pearl opens another anemone.

Follow the moon. Gather six pearls to awaken a passage. Dive through the shallows, a golden particle tide, and a deep star nursery. A completed journey begins again.

Sweep the water. Drag empty water to make a current. It carries the whole school and ripples through their tendrils. Hold empty water briefly to call the school closer.

Stretch & share. Pull a tendril and release its elastic ribbon. Drag a bell toward another friend to exchange colors and nourish the reef. Separate them before they can share again.
